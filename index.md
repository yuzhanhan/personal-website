Welcome! I am an applied microeconomist whose research lies at the intersection of environmental, urban, and labor economics. My research examines how environmental conditions affect economic activity and well-being, how workers adjust to environmental risks, and how firm decisions and infrastructure networks shape the distribution of environmental costs. A second strand studies employer wage-setting power and the measurement of productivity in high-skilled labor markets. See the [Research](./research) page for more information on my ongoing projects.
